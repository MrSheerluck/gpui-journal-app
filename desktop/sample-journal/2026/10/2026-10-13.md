# First light

The window was **still dark** when I sat down, and the flat smelled of *yesterday's coffee*. I meant to write one line and kept going until the kettle clicked.

## What I want from this journal

Not a ledger. A **place to think slowly** — the kind of page that doesn't ask me to be clever. Some days that will be three words; ~~some days it will be an essay~~, or a list of groceries. *Both count.*

I'm keeping a few conventions, lightly worn: `#tiny` for moments worth keeping, and links when something deserves one — the [long now](https://longnow.org/ideas/) piece from Sunday, for instance, has been following me around all week.

### A note on mornings

There's a particular hour before the city starts — the light comes in low and everything in the kitchen looks *borrowed*. I read a line in a poem this morning: "the day is a door left ajar". I don't know who wrote it. I wrote it here anyway.
