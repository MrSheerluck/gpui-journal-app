# Small systems

I've been rebuilding the morning in small pieces, mostly because the big pieces keep failing.

## The pieces

- Water before caffeine
- Ten minutes of writing, no screen
  - actually the screen, but only the page
- A short walk that ends at the good bakery
- Two pages of reading, paper preferred

## The order matters

1. Kettle on
2. Sit down before the water boils — this is the whole trick
3. Write until the cup is empty
4. Walk, then work
