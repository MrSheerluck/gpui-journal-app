# Borrowed lines

Things I read this week and did not want to lose.

> The most regretful people on earth are those who felt the call to creative work, who felt their own creative power restive and uprising, and gave to it neither power nor time.
>
> — Mary Oliver

This one I keep arguing with:

> **Write, don't edit.** Editing is a way of pretending the work is already there.
>
> > Which is itself a way of pretending the work is *not* there.

Other things, unquoted:

- A kitchen timer for reading — twenty minutes, no negotiation
- *The summer slipping away*, a phrase that keeps returning
- The corner fold: marking a page without dog-earing it
