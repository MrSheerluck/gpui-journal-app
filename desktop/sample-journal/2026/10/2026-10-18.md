# A quiet Sunday

The rain started at seven and stayed all day, the polite kind that hums on the awning and never commits. We did the crossword on the floor with the dog between us, and I lost the argument about 12-across on a technicality involving the word "erg".

"You can't just *decide* it's erg," she said.

— It's four letters and it means work.

— It's Sunday.

Fair. Café at half past ten, where the waiter corrected my pronunciation of *pain au chocolat* with the patience of a man who has done it a thousand times. I wrote two lines about the fog and one about her laugh, and the rest of the page is a shopping list, which feels honest.

Things heard on the walk back:

- A door slamming in a rhythm that was almost music
- Someone practising scales, badly, happily
- 日本語 from the upstairs window — a lesson, or a recording of one

**Still raining.** Good.
