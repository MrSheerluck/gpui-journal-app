## A long paragraph, for wrapping

There is a particular pleasure in watching a long sentence find the right edge of the measure — the wrap landing where you hoped it would, the line above it snug, the paragraph as a whole reading like a small argument you won by accident. This paragraph exists to be that: one thought, carried past the fold, so the wrapping, the leading, and the rhythm of the serif face have somewhere to show themselves off. If it does its job, you should stop noticing it somewhere in the middle.
